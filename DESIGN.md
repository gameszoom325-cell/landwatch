## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-stakes institutional paradigm: ministerial editorial gravitas fused with contemporary predictive SaaS precision. Built for national infrastructure monitoring, statutory tracking, and predictive land acquisition analytics, the system prioritizes uncompromising clarity, constitutional authority, and operational density.

The aesthetic fuses classic government publication design (typographic restraint, structured rules, clear hierarchy) with modern data visualization (dense tabular grids, risk-calibrated status markers, predictive confidence gauges). The emotional tone is decisive, sober, and unyielding—fostering rapid cognitive processing for district collectors, ministry secretaries, and field officers making high-capital, high-consequence interventions.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative light-mode baseline rooted in sovereign deep navies and structural slates, engineered to maintain AAA contrast across all critical data surfaces.

- **Primary (`#1B3A5B`) & Core Navy (`#0F2C4C`):** Used for institutional headers, high-level structural sidebars, dominant action buttons, and primary data summaries.
- **Surface Foundations:** Background layers leverage pristine pure white (`#FFFFFF`) for card modules, layered against muted ministerial slate tints (`#F8FAFC` base application background, `#F1F5F9` nested inset panels and data wells).
- **Structural Outlines:** `#E2E8F0` provides crisp, hairline perimeter definition across all table rows, dividers, and card borders.
- **Statutory & Predictive Status Accents:**
  - *High-Risk / Statutory Breach:* Crimson Red (`#EB5757`) with soft tinted background (`#FEF2F2`) and deep contrast text (`#991B1B`).
  - *Imminent Delay / Moderate Risk:* Warm Amber (`#F2994A`) paired with background (`#FFFBEB`) and deep text (`#92400E`).
  - *Compliant / On-Track:* Forest Emerald (`#27AE60`) with background (`#F0FDF4`) and deep text (`#166534`).
  - *Statutory Information / Baseline Notice:* Civic Cyan (`#2D9CDB`) with background (`#F0F9FF`) and deep text (`#075985`).

## Typography

The typographic pairing reflects dual operational pillars: legislative authority and mathematical fidelity.

- **Editorial Headings (`IBM Plex Serif`):** Conveys the permanence and legal weight of government gazettes, gazetted notifications, and ministerial briefings. Used for module headings, macro-metric overviews, and executive summaries.
- **Precision Body & Metadata (`Hanken Grotesk`):** Modern, direct, and unencumbered. Engineered for complex multi-column grids, spatial coordinates, and statutory milestone labels.
- **Tabular Numerics Rule:** All numbers across data tables, statistical tiles, coordinates, and latency indicators must enable `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1` to ensure vertical column alignment across rapidly updating figures.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column adaptive grid operating with high spatial efficiency. The system relies on a predictable 8px/4px base scale, avoiding superfluous padding to maximize visible statutory data above the viewport fold.

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column framework, 24px margins, 24px gutters. Analytical split layouts permit fixed 280px sidebar navigation, a 60% macro data workspace, and a 40% contextual statutory drawer.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout, 24px margins, 16px gutters. Analytical side-panels collapse to bottom sheets or slide-over overlays.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout, 16px margins, 12px gutters. Data tables switch to stacked comparative inspection cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy prioritizes crisp perimeter definition over theatrical shadow layering. Surfaces rely primarily on low-contrast structural outlines combined with tinted, low-opacity ambient shadows.

- **Base Layer (Flat):** Neutral canvas `#F8FAFC` hosts elevated card structures.
- **Level 1 (Cards, Stat Tiles, Table Enclosures):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, shadow `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 44, 76, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 44, 76, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States, Active Filter Menus, Flyouts):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, shadow `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 44, 76, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 44, 76, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Alerts, Statutory Override Dialogs):** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #94A3B8`, shadow `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 44, 76, 0.12), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 44, 76, 0.08)`.
- **Inverted Depth (Wells & Code blocks):** Background `#F1F5F9`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, inner shadow `inset 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 44, 76, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The shape architecture is restrained and disciplined (`roundedness: 1`), embodying bureaucratic permanence and legal integrity rather than playful consumer softness.

- **Default Radii:** Micro elements (buttons, badge tags, text fields, table cells) feature a tight `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- **Container Radii:** Main dashboard cards, statutory progression containers, and metric tiles take `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Overlays & Dialogs:** Large modal surfaces leverage `0.75rem` (12px). Full pill rounding (`9999px`) is reserved exclusively for high-visibility operational status pips and compact metric indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#1B3A5B` fill with `#FFFFFF` text, `4px` border radius, padding `8px 16px`. Active state shifts to `#0F2C4C`. Focus ring: `2px solid #2D9CDB` with `2px` offset.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#1B3A5B`. Hover: `#F8FAFC`.
- **Destructive/High Risk:** Solid `#EB5757` or outlined `1px solid #EB5757` with `#991B1B` text on `#FEF2F2`.

### Chips & Badges
- Constructed with high-contrast text and low-intensity tinted backdrops.
- Height: `22px`, font: `11px` bold uppercase tracking (`0.05em`).
- Red status: `#FEF2F2` background, `#EB5757` border (`1px`), `#991B1B` text. Includes an inline `6px` solid status circle.
- Amber status: `#FFFBEB` background, `#F2994A` border, `#92400E` text.
- Green status: `#F0FDF4` background, `#27AE60` border, `#166534` text.

### Stat Tiles
- Built for real-time risk intelligence. Surface `#FFFFFF`, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `16px` padding.
- Displays label in `Hanken Grotesk` uppercase (`11px`, `#64748B`), large primary metric in `IBM Plex Serif` (`28px`, tabular figures), and statutory deviation trend indicator at the base.

### Govtech Data Tables
- Header row: `#F8FAFC` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0` bottom border, `11px` uppercase slate text (`#475569`).
- Data rows: `#FFFFFF` surface, alternating `#FAFCFE` optional striping for massive datasets, `12px` vertical cell padding.
- Text: Tabular sans-serif with right-aligned numeric data and status chips left-aligned.

### Statutory Progress Timeline
- Linear progression bar displaying mandatory acquisition steps (Section 4 Preliminary Notification, Section 11 Survey, Section 19 Declaration, Section 25 Award).
- Milestones feature numbered 20px circular nodes:
  - Completed: Solid `#1B3A5B` node with white checkmark.
  - Delayed/Critical: Solid `#EB5757` node with amber warning pulse.
  - Upcoming: Hollow `#CBD5E1` node with slate interior.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF`, border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`, radius: `4px`, height: `38px`, padding: `0 12px`.
- Active/Focus: Border `#1B3A5B`, ring: `1px solid #1B3A5B`.
- Helper and error text: strictly aligned below inputs in `12px` font.
