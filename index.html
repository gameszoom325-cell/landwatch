The Core Dilemma

Where Delays Happen in Land Acquisition

Delays in government land acquisition cases usually surface only after deadlines have already passed, causing citizen protests, stalled infrastructure, and costly court disputes.

notification_important

1. Late Warning

Officials only find out when statutory deadlines expire, making fixes twice as hard and forcing cases back to square one.

Impact: Expired legal windows & lapsed notices
folder_off

2. Buried Reasons

Files pass across multiple revenue departments without clear root-cause visibility. Officers can’t tell which desk or boundary issue is causing the stall.

Impact: Months spent tracking down paperwork
gavel

3. Reactive Rushes

Emergency court stays and compounding delay interest penalties could easily have been prevented with 30 days of proactive notice.

Impact: Litigation freezes & spiraling public costs
