The Statutory Cascading Trap of RFCTLARR 2013

When preliminary notifications expire under Section 19(1) or Section 25, the entire legal acquisition lapses by operation of law. Government agencies must re-initiate surveys from scratch—costing taxpayers billions in revised market multipliers, interest penalties under Section 80, and citizen displacement distress.

5-Stage Continuous Data & Inference Pipeline

01. Ingestion

DILRMP Integration

Extracts revenue records, cadastral surveys, and notice dates from state revenue databases.

02. Engineering

Feature Extraction

Computes tenure fragmentation, disputed co-shares, survey officer caseload, and local land type.

03. Dual ML

XGBoost Inference

Model A predicts delay probability (0–100%); Model B forecasts delay extent (in days).

04. XAI Synthesis

SHAP Explanation

Deconstructs model output into human-verifiable statutory driver weights for each parcel.

05. Action Alerts

Automated Dockets

Dispatches prioritized intervention briefs directly to District Land Acquisition Officers (DLAO).

Empirical & Statutory Foundations

Evidence based. Policy aligned.

LandWatch synthesizes statutory administrative law with peer-reviewed machine learning architectures.

[REF 01 · STATUTE] Ministry of Law & Justice

The RFCTLARR Act, 2013 (Act 30 of 2013)

Provides mandatory procedural milestones, Section 11 publication windows, Section 19(1) declaration caps, and Section 25 award timelines.

[REF 02 · POLICY] DoLR / MoRD

DILRMP Technical Guidelines & Cadastral Standards

Digital India Land Records Modernization Programme standards for spatial vector mapping, RoR integration, and sub-registrar deeds.

[REF 03 · EMPIRICAL] NITI Aayog / CAG

CAG Performance Audit of Major Highway & Railway Corridors

Empirical identification that land acquisition and Right of Way (RoW) disputes account for >62% of all capital infrastructure delays.

[REF 04 · ML ENGINE] KDD 2016

Chen & Guestrin (2016) — XGBoost

A Scalable Tree Boosting System. Formulates the core regression and classification backend for tabular administrative casework.

[REF 05 · EXPLAINABILITY] NeurIPS 2017

Lundberg & Lee (2017) — Unified Approach to SHAP

Provides game-theoretic Shapley Additive Explanations (TreeSHAP) to attribute statutory delay likelihoods to individual procedural lags.

India Enterprise Architecture (IndEA 2.0)

MeitY framework ensuring seamless interoperability across National, State, and District government digital application registries.
